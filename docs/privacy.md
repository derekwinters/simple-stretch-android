# Simple Stretch privacy policy

Effective date: 21 October 2026

Simple Stretch is a stretch reminder app for Android made by Derek Winters. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: everything stays on your phone, and nothing is sent to the developer or anyone else.

## What the app stores

To work, the app keeps the following on your device, in its private storage:

- the stretches in your library (names, instructions and durations)
- your schedules and reminder times, and which stretches each reminder includes
- your daily goals
- the stretches you log as done, with the date and time
- the days you choose to skip
- a few internal settings, such as which reminder alarms are set and whether you have closed the skip card for today

## What the app collects and shares

Nothing. Simple Stretch does not have permission to use the internet. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising, crash reporting or third-party tracking code. The developer never receives any of your data, and none of it is sold or shared.

## Permissions

- **Notifications**: to show your stretch reminders.
- **Alarms and reminders**: to deliver reminders at the times you set.
- **Run at startup**: to set your reminders again after the phone restarts or the time or time zone changes.

## Device backup

If you have Android's backup turned on, Android may include the app's data in your device backup to your Google account. That backup is handled by Android and Google under Google's privacy policy; the developer cannot see it. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

## Deleting your data

Clear the app's storage in your phone's settings, or uninstall the app. Either removes all of the data listed above from your device.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
