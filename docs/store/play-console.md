# Play Console listing details

The parts of the Google Play listing that are not text written in this repository: they are
chosen from Play Console's own lists or are contact details. The owner enters them in Play
Console by hand; nothing in this repository uploads them, and the release run neither reads
nor checks them. This page records what was decided, so the choice is reviewed in a pull
request like the rest of the listing. The listing's title and descriptions are in
[Store listing text](listing.md).

## App category

- **App or game:** App
- **Category:** Health & Fitness

Proposed, not confirmed. Play Console offers a fixed list of tags for each category, and that

shows for Health & Fitness (at most five), and update this page to the names chosen.
