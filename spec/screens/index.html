Screens

The app has five top-level screens, reached from a bottom navigation bar (HOME-004): Home, Schedules, Goals, Trends and Settings. The approved mockups fix which tabs exist, their order and labels, what each screen holds and where, and the no-goals and skipped-day states; they do not fix icons, colours, type, spacing or the figure drawing, which follow the app's theme (StretchTheme, including dynamic colour).

Home

  • HOME-001 The home screen shows, from the top: the banners (HOME-005), today's date, the goal ring (HOME-006) with "Stretch now" under it (HOME-007), and the Today list (HOME-002). There is no schedule list (HOME-010 is the Schedules tab) and no skip card. See the mockups ("Home", "Home, no goals", "Home, today skipped").
  • HOME-002 The Today list: today's reminders from all enabled schedules, each with the time, stretches and schedule name. Upcoming reminders come first, in time order; below them, after a divider, today's past reminders, in time order and greyed out. A reminder at or before the current time is past. A repeating schedule shows as one row at its next slot today, with its repeat summary and how many slots are left today, among the upcoming rows; once its last slot today has passed it moves to the greyed past rows, at that last slot. When today is skipped, every row is greyed, upcoming and past alike (the two groups keep their order). With no reminders today the list says so. The "Today" heading row carries a text button at its right end (SKIP-001): "Skip today" while today is not skipped (enabled when at least one reminder is left today, shown disabled when none are), and "Resume today" once today is skipped. This is the only place on Home to skip or resume today. Every row opens the stretch session when tapped (HOME-009).

Invariant — When today is skipped, the Today heading always shows "Resume today". No state hides it: not an empty Today list, not the time of day, and nothing stored per device.

Invariant — Which rows are upcoming and which are past is a pure function of the rows and the time read from the app clock (SCHED-016), never of when the screen was opened, so a reminder moves below the divider at its time while Home is shown. - HOME-003 Retired. It described the schedule list on the home screen, which moved, with its switches and its invariant, to the Schedules tab (HOME-010). - HOME-004 A bottom navigation bar (Material 3 NavigationBar) is shown on each of the five top-level screens, with five items in this order: Home, Schedules, Goals, Trends, Settings. Every item has an icon and its text label, always shown; the selected item is highlighted. Each top-level screen is one tap from every other. Screens opened from a top-level screen (the schedule editor, skipped days, my stretches, a trends day, the stretch session) cover the whole window and have no bar; Back returns from them. How screens and the bar move between one another is HOME-012.

Back on any top-level screen other than Home returns to Home; Back on Home leaves the app. Switching tabs keeps each tab's own state (such as its scroll position) and never stacks one tab on another.

The top bar has no icon actions and no overflow menu. Home's top bar title is the app's name, "Simple Stretch", read from app_name (STORE-LIST-008); each other top-level screen's title is its own name ("Schedules", "Daily goals", "Trends", "Settings"), with no back arrow.

Invariant — Every bar item is labelled. No tab is an unlabelled icon, and the labels show whether or not the item is selected.

Invariant — The stretch library is reached by a labelled item (Settings, HOME-011), never only by an unlabelled icon. - HOME-005 Banners appear at the top of Home, and only on Home, when notifications are blocked (NOTIF-005) or exact alarms are not allowed (SCHED-007). - HOME-006 Below the date, the goal ring: the home screen widget's segmented ring (WIDGET-002 to WIDGET-006), with one arc per current goal (GOAL-008), each filling with that goal's progress today, and in its centre the launcher icon's side-bend figure and today's count "done/total" (or a check mark once every goal is met), exactly as the widget draws them. With no goals the ring is the widget's dotted empty track with the dimmed figure, and a "Set a goal" button beside "Stretch now" opens the Goals tab. The ring is drawn in the app's theme colours (primary for the arcs, onSurface for the text and check mark), except the centre figure and its mat, which are always the launcher icon's colours (ic_launcher_foreground.xml): the figure (body and head) #1361A7, and when dimmed #1361A7 at 40%; the mat #818589 in every state. This holds in light and dark mode, with dynamic colour on or off. Its content description says the count (e.g. "Today's goals: 4 of 8 done").

Invariant — Home's ring and the widget never disagree. Both are built by the same GoalRing.fromLog from GoalMath.goalRows (WIDGET-009) and drawn by the same renderer; Home has no ring maths of its own.

Invariant — The ring's centre figure and mat never take a theme or wallpaper colour. They come from one shared definition of the launcher icon's colours, in every theme, mode and state. - HOME-007 A "Stretch now" button under the ring opens the stretch session screen (SESS-001) without waiting for a reminder. - HOME-008 Retired. It described the closable "Skip today" card, which was removed with its close (X) button and its stored closed date. Its invariant, a skipped day is never hidden, now lives in HOME-002. - HOME-009 Tapping any row of the Today list (HOME-002) opens the stretch session screen (SESS-001) as that reminder's notification would (NOTIF-002). Every row is tappable: past, upcoming and current alike, and also while today is skipped. A set-time row opens the session with its reminder's named stretches, its schedule name and the row's time today; a repeating schedule's row, whose reminders name no stretches (SCHED-011), opens it with no named list ("Goals still short today"), as its notification does. Tapping only opens the screen: nothing is checked or logged until Save (SESS-002, SESS-003), and rows look the same whether or not their stretches were logged.

Invariant — A Today row opens exactly what its notification would. The row and the notification build the session route with the same function from the same values (stretch ids in the reminder's order, schedule name, scheduled time), so the two ways in cannot drift apart.

Schedules

  • HOME-010 The Schedules tab, titled "Schedules", lists every schedule with its name, its days and its times (or repeat summary, e.g. "Every 60 min at :50, 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM") and an enabled switch. Tapping a schedule opens its editor; a "New schedule" button creates one. At the top, a labelled "Skip other days" button opens the skipped-days screen (SKIP-002). With no schedules it invites the user to create one. (It replaces HOME-003.)

Invariant — Toggling a schedule's switch reschedules immediately; there is no separate save step for it.

Settings

  • HOME-011 The Settings tab, titled "Settings", holds a list of labelled items. For now it has one: "My stretches", with the subtitle "Add, rename or remove stretches", which opens the stretch library (LIB-006).

Navigation motion

  • HOME-012 Navigation motion follows Material's patterns, 300 ms in all, with the outgoing screen's fade taking the first 35% (105 ms) and the incoming screen's fade the rest (195 ms), so each fade ends before the next begins:
  • Tab to tab (from the bar, from Home's "Set a goal", or Back from a tab to Home): Material fade through. The outgoing tab fades out; then the incoming tab fades in while scaling up from 92% to full size. The bar does not move or fade; only its selected item changes.
  • Tab to a full-window screen (the schedule editor, skipped days, my stretches, a trends day, the stretch session, including one opened from a notification or the widget, NOTIF-002, NOTIF-003, WIDGET-007, and one full-window screen replacing another): Material shared axis X, forward. The tab screen and its bar slide 30 dp to the left and fade out, while the new screen slides in from 30 dp to the right, fading in once the tab screen has gone, and covers the whole window.
  • Back from a full-window screen (Back, its back arrow, Save or Done, or the widget opening Home): the same motion in reverse. The full-window screen slides right and fades out, and the tab screen comes back from the left with its bar. A predictive back gesture drives the screens' motion where the system supports it: the screens follow the gesture, and the bar starts returning when the gesture completes and finishes with its tab screen (the known exception below).

Routes, tab state saving and the HOME-004 Back rules do not change. The motion is chosen from the kinds of the two screens (tab or full-window) and the direction, by one shared definition used by every destination.

Invariant — No navigation uses the library's default cross-fade, and no two screens are ever shown part-transparent at the same time. Every destination sets its own enter, exit, pop-enter and pop-exit motion.

Invariant — The bar never moves during a tab switch, and never disappears on its own: it leaves and returns only with its tab screen, and the area the screens are drawn in does not change height when it does.

Known exception, tracked as a follow-up in #108: while a predictive back gesture is held, the tab screen shows without its bar; the bar starts returning only when the gesture completes, and finishes with its tab screen.

Build checklist

  • [x] Home state is derived from Room flows plus a 30-second tick that reads the app clock (HOME-002, SCHED-016).
  • [x] Today rows' past state is unit tested with a fixed clock (HOME-002).
  • [x] The Today list's split into upcoming and greyed past rows, and its greying when today is skipped, is a pure, unit-tested function, including a repeating schedule moving to the past rows after its last slot (HOME-002).
  • [x] Banners re-check permissions on resume (HOME-005).
  • [x] The exact-alarm banner is the only place the user is asked for exact alarms: no prompt at first launch or on first schedule save (HOME-005, SCHED-007).
  • [x] Banners are drawn by Home's content only; a Robolectric test finds them on Home and not on the other tabs (HOME-005).
  • [x] Today heading: a "Skip today" / "Resume today" text button at its right, disabled when nothing is left today; its state is a pure, unit-tested function, and a skipped day gives "Resume today" even with an empty list (HOME-002).
  • [x] No skip-card closed date is written or read (HOME-008 retired).
  • [x] Bottom bar: five labelled items in the order Home, Schedules, Goals, Trends, Settings; tab navigation pops up to Home with saved state, restores state and is single-top; a Robolectric test taps every tab from every other and checks the labels (HOME-004).
  • [x] Back from every other tab returns to Home; on Home the navigation host holds no Back callback, so Back leaves the app (Robolectric test, HOME-004).
  • [x] No top-bar icon actions and no overflow menu on any top-level screen (HOME-004).
  • [x] Every destination sets explicit enter, exit, pop-enter and pop-exit transitions from one shared definition; a unit test checks that each fade ends before the next begins, and a Robolectric test with the clock paused checks that a tab switch is over well inside the library's 700 ms and that a full-window screen slides in from the right and back out to it (HOME-012 first invariant).
  • [x] The bar is drawn once, outside the navigation host; tab screens reserve its space themselves, so the host never changes height. A Robolectric test with the clock paused checks that the bar holds still through a tab switch and slides with its tab screen when a full-window screen opens or closes, including one opened from outside the UI (HOME-012 second invariant).
  • [x] The Home top bar's title is stringResource(R.string.app_name), never a literal; a Robolectric test renders Home and finds the app_name text (HOME-004, STORE-LIST-008 invariant).
  • [x] The ring is GoalRing.fromLog drawn by GoalRingRenderer into the Compose canvas with the theme's colours, and the figure, dimmed figure and mat in the launcher icon's colours from the shared definition (unit tested, HOME-006 invariant); its done/total and empty state are unit tested (HOME-006 invariant).
  • [x] Goal progress re-queries when the date changes (the clock tick drives the day bounds) (HOME-006).
  • [x] "Stretch now" opens the session and "Set a goal" opens the Goals tab (Robolectric test, HOME-006, HOME-007).
  • [x] Today rows build their session route with the same pure, unit-tested function as the notification intent, from the reminder's stretch ids, schedule name and today's time (HOME-009 invariant).
  • [x] "Skip other days" on Schedules and "My stretches" on Settings open their screens (Robolectric test, HOME-010, HOME-011).
  • [x] Store listing reviewed: this page is store: omit, and any claim in docs/store/listing.md that cites it is still true (STORE-LIST-002, STORE-LIST-003).
