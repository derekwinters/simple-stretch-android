Widgets

A home screen widget shows today's goals at a glance. The segmented ring is the first widget; the mat-bar and goal-list widgets are later alternatives.

Segmented ring

  • WIDGET-001 The widget is 2×2 cells by default and resizable in both directions. It is drawn square, as large as the smaller side allows, centred in its cell area. It has no widget background: only the disc (WIDGET-005), the ring and the centre are drawn.
  • WIDGET-002 The ring has one arc per current goal (GOAL-008), in goals-screen order (GOAL-002: by stretch name, ignoring case, ties by id), clockwise from 12 o'clock. The arcs share the ring equally with a gap of 8° between neighbours; one goal is a full ring with no gap. Arc ends are round, and the 8° is the visible gap between the round ends. Each arc is a track in the track colour with the filled part drawn over it.
  • WIDGET-003 Each arc fills clockwise from its start by that goal's progress / count (GOAL-003), capped at full: 4 of 3 is a full arc. The fill is continuous (2 of 3 is two thirds of the arc), and in-progress and met arcs use the same colour.
  • WIDGET-004 The centre shows the launcher icon's side-bend figure on its mat and, under it, today's count as "done/total": total is the sum of the current goals' counts and done the sum of each goal's progress capped at its count (so 4 of 3 adds 3). Once every goal is met, the count is replaced by a plain check mark in the text colour, with no circle or badge.
  • WIDGET-005 Behind the ring is a disc in the disc colour at 55% opacity, reaching the ring's inner edge, so the widget reads on black and on light wallpapers.
  • WIDGET-006 States:
  • Nothing done yet (at least one goal, done = 0): the figure is dimmed and the arcs show only their track; the count reads "0/total".
  • No goals set: the ring is a dotted track (round dots in the track colour, no arcs), the figure is dimmed and centred, and there is no count and no check mark.
  • Otherwise the figure is drawn in the arc colour.

"Dimmed" means the figure (body and head) is drawn in the text colour at 40% opacity; the mat keeps its colour. - WIDGET-007 Tapping the widget opens the app on its home screen (HOME-001): any screen open above home is closed.

Colour

  • WIDGET-008 The widget follows the system light/dark mode. Where the phone supports dynamic colour (Android 12 and later, the same test as the app's theme) it uses the wallpaper colours, with the roles Material 3's dynamic schemes use:
Part Role Light Dark
Arcs and figure primary system_accent1_600 system_accent1_200
Disc (at 55%) surface system_neutral1_10 system_neutral1_900
Text and check mark onSurface system_neutral1_900 system_neutral1_100
Empty track onSurface at 14% (light) / 18% (dark)
Mat outline system_neutral2_500 system_neutral2_400

Without dynamic colour it uses the mockup's fixed values:

Part Light Dark
Arcs and figure #1361A7 #3D86D1
Disc (at 55%) white #121212
Text and check mark #1C1B1F #F5F5F5
Empty track black at 14% white at 18%
Mat #818589 #818589

Invariant — The fixed mockup colours are used only when dynamic colour is unavailable. The choice between dynamic and fixed is made in one place, by the same Android-version test as the app's theme; no part of the widget mixes the two.

Both the light and the dark drawing are produced at every redraw and the launcher shows the one for the current mode, so switching between light and dark needs no redraw. A change of wallpaper colours shows at the next redraw (WIDGET-010).

Data and refresh

  • WIDGET-009 The widget's goals and progress come from the same calculation as the goals screen (GoalMath.goalRows, GOAL-002/003): current goals only (GOAL-008), with progress counted from the completion log over today's local calendar day.

Invariant — Progress is derived from the completion log, never stored (GOAL-004). The widget keeps no counter and no copy of progress between redraws; each redraw reads the log.

Invariant — Only today's completions count. Each redraw counts completions from local midnight today to the next local midnight (GoalMath.dayBounds), never a rolling 24 hours or a day fixed when the widget was added. - WIDGET-010 The widget redraws: - after every logged completion (SESS-003); - after every goal add, change or removal (GOAL-009), and after a stretch is renamed or deleted (which changes the arcs' order or removes a goal, LIB-005); - at local midnight, through an alarm set for the start of the next local day at each redraw; - when it is added or resized, when the app starts, after a reboot, a time or time-zone change, or an app update.

Invariant — The widget never shows a number the app would not. Every write that can change today's goals or progress (completions, goals, stretches) is followed by a redraw, made from the repository, not from a screen, so no screen can forget it; and the midnight alarm is re-armed at every redraw, so a new day always starts at 0.

Build checklist

  • [x] GoalRing.model is pure and unit tested for 0, 1, 3 and 8 goals; none, partly and all met; progress above the count; and no goals (WIDGET-002 … WIDGET-006).
  • [x] GoalRing.fromLog builds the ring from GoalMath.goalRows, the goals screen's calculation, and drops stretches without a goal (WIDGET-009).
  • [x] GoalRing.nextRedrawAt is the next local midnight, including across a daylight-saving change (WIDGET-010).
  • [x] WidgetPalette.select returns the fixed mockup values only when dynamic colour is unavailable, and its fixed values match WIDGET-008 (unit tested).
  • [x] The renderer draws round arc ends inside each arc's share, so the visible gap is 8° (WIDGET-002).
  • [x] StretchRepository requests a redraw after logCompletions, setGoal, removeGoal, saveStretch and deleteStretch (WIDGET-010 invariant).
  • [x] The midnight alarm is re-armed at every redraw and cancelled when the last widget is removed (WIDGET-010).
  • [x] Tapping opens home with any screen above it closed (WIDGET-007).
  • [ ] On a device: light and dark, dynamic colour on and off, resize, tap, and the midnight reset (human-only issue).
  • [x] Store listing reviewed: every claim in docs/store/listing.md that cites this page is still true, and at least one does (store: headline; STORE-LIST-003, STORE-LIST-004).
