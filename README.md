# Simple Stretch

A small Android app that reminds you to stretch during the day, on your own schedule.

The documentation, covering features, building and installing, releases, the behaviour
specification and design decisions, is published at
<https://derekwinters.github.io/simple-stretch-android/>. Its sources are the Markdown files
under [docs/](docs/index.md).

The [privacy policy](https://derekwinters.github.io/simple-stretch-android/privacy/) (source:
[docs/privacy.md](docs/privacy.md)) explains that the app keeps all its data on the device.
